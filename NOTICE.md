# What the MIT licence here does and does not cover

`LICENSE` is the unmodified MIT text, on purpose. It used to carry this note at
the bottom, and GitHub then labelled the repository "License" instead of "MIT
license" — its detector needs a verbatim match, and an appended paragraph is
enough to lose it. A licence nobody can identify is worse than one with its
exceptions written next door.

## Covered by MIT

All of the source code: `site/index.html`, everything in `tools/`, and
`tools-harness.js`. Take it, learn from it, ship it.

## Not covered

**The written content.** The case studies, the copy, the section titles. It is
a portfolio; the writing is the work.

**The imagery in `assets/` and `assets/case/`.** Some is generated by
`tools/gerar-visuais.py` and `tools/gerar-glaze-thumb.py` — those *scripts* are
MIT, so run them and make your own. The rendered output belongs to this
portfolio.

**The screenshots of the Rock in Rio Lisboa website.** These document work
carried out for that organisation and remain their property. They are here to
illustrate my contribution, not to be redistributed.

**The visual design.** Layout, typography, colour, motion, and the way they go
together.

## In short

Read the code, borrow the techniques, copy the approach. Please don't
republish the content, the imagery or the design as your own.
